GRAMMAR BLUEPRINT 1

How to say "O Mais" in English? (Short Adjectives)

1. SHORT ADJECTIVES

THE + ADJ + EST

We use this to compare 1 person with everybody else.

Tim is the tallest.

2. IRREGULAR

DIFFERENT WORDS

Some adjectives change completely! Good / Bad.

Good = The best

Bad = The worst

TEACHER: Ask the student to read the examples out loud.

GRAMMAR BLUEPRINT 2

How to say "O Mais" in English? (Long Adjectives)

1. LONG ADJECTIVES

THE MOST + ADJ

Use for adjectives with 2 or more syllables (Beautiful, Expensive).

This car is the most expensive.

TEACHER: Explain that we don't say "Beautifulest" or "Expensivest". We use "The most".
